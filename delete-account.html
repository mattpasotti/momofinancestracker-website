Delete your account

Last updated: 13 October 2026

You can delete your Momo account at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Momo and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete account.
  3. Read the summary, export a copy of your data if you want one, then type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have Momo installed, email support@momofinancestracker.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We will delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. We may first ask you to confirm that the address is yours.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and every sign-in method attached to it: email and password, Google, Apple.
  • Your email address and the authentication records held by our authentication provider.
  • All your financial data, both the copy in your account and the one on the device you delete from: transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and loans.

What is not deleted

  • An active Plus subscription is not cancelled. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so deleting your account does not stop billing. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Records already on your other devices. Any other phone signed in to the account is signed out, but the records already on it stay there until someone signs in on it or the app is uninstalled.

Export first if you want a copy

Deleting erases the copy on your device and the one in your account, so there is nothing left for us to restore. You can export everything to CSV first from Settings → Export & Import. Export is free for everyone.

Questions

Write to support@momofinancestracker.com and we will help.
